About Konkan

Welcome to Tourist Place Konkan — your gateway to the breathtaking beauty of India's western coast. Konkan is a region of stunning contrasts — from golden beaches and blue seas to lush green forests and charming villages.

Explore the tranquil beaches of Tarkarli, Kashid, and Ganpatipule, or dive into history at the majestic Sindhudurg and Kolaba Forts. The region is also home to ancient temples, waterfalls, and the mouthwatering Malvani cuisine that captures the true essence of coastal life.

Whether you seek adventure, peace, or culture — Konkan welcomes you with open arms and endless beauty.

Top Tourist Places in Konkan

🏖 Tarkarli Beach

Enjoy water sports, scuba diving, and stunning sunsets by the Arabian Sea.

🏰 Sindhudurg Fort

A historic sea fort built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, rich with stories of valor.

🌅 Ganpatipule Temple

A serene seaside temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha — a blend of faith and beauty.

🌿 Dapoli & Ratnagiri

Explore coastal towns known for beaches, Alphonso mangoes, and natural charm.
